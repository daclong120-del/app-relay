# Birthday calendar

**Developer**: DevWolf
**Rating**: 4.8
**Installs**: N/A

## Description

Organizes all of your birthdays. See all at a glace and get reminded when you want!

✔ Year view: Simple year overview to quickly spot everything.

✔ Reminder:  Set several reminders on the days leading up to the birthday when you want to be notified. You can also specify the time.

✔ Widget: Access your upcoming birthdays right from your device&#39;s home screen with our convenient widget!

✔ Import:  Import from your contacts or via Excel (.xlsx/.csv) file.

✔ Fast creation:  Create multiple birthdays using simple date selection by scrolling.

✔ Share:  Share your birthdays with your family and friends as calendar (.ical) or Excel (.csv) file.
This file can be imported into the app or the device calendar.

✔ Export:  Write all birthdays in one of your calendars or save them as Excel (.csv) file.

✔ Synchronize:  Synchronizes your birthdays with a calendars. If another person has access to the calendar you can manage them together!
